# Privacy and responsible use

Road-camera footage can identify people, vehicles, routes, homes and daily
routines. Generated clips concentrate those observations and may be more
sensitive than the original archive.

Before running or publishing this pipeline:

- understand the recording, retention and disclosure rules in your jurisdiction;
- keep source media read-only and access-controlled;
- use short retention periods where possible;
- redact faces, number plates and location overlays before public sharing;
- do not publish raw manifests, failed-attempt evidence or OCR frames without review;
- label machine-detected clips as candidates rather than proven legal violations;
- manually review evidence before making safety or enforcement claims.

The detector estimates likely pass events from visual trajectories. It does not
measure legal passing distance, determine intent or establish fault. Perspective,
camera placement and lens distortion make metric distance estimation a separate
calibration problem.
